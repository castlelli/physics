Physics

Mechanics projects written in Python, each with a version that runs in the browser.

  • Force system

    Resultant, moments, invariant and central axis of a system of forces on a rigid body, in 3D.

    physics/wrench

  • Moment field

    The moment of a force about every pole, in 3D: the symmetry about the line of action and the sections that prove it.

    physics/momentfield

  • Duffing

    The Duffing oscillator live: RK4 integration in the browser, Poincaré section, bifurcations and basins of attraction. In Portuguese.

    physics/duffing

  • Carousel

    An ant walks on a turning carousel: law of motion, velocities and accelerations, including the Coriolis one.

    physics/carousel

  • Hypocycloid

    The curve traced by a point of a disc rolling inside a ring, with radius of curvature, Frenet frame and kinematics.

    physics/hypocycloid
