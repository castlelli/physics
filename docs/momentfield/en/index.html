The moment field of a force, in 3D

M(O) = (P − O) ∧ F turns around the force's line of action. Change the force, drag the figure and watch the symmetry hold.

Section normal to the axis

Only the poles of one plane normal to the line of action, seen along it: the force points at you. The moments turn around the axis like a pinwheel. Move the plane along the axis: the drawing does not change.

Section containing the axis

Now only the poles of the plane that contains the line of action. The moments are normal to that plane: they go in on one side of the axis and come out on the other, larger the farther away.

The browser checks the symmetry

Five hundred random poles around P. For each, the four properties that make the field axisymmetric, measured here and in the report's Python. The errors are the size of machine rounding.
