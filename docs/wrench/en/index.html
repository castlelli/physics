Central axis

On the central axis, the system's moment is parallel to the resultant and as small as it gets. There the whole system becomes R and a couple M_E in the same direction.

Why this axis

Move the pole P along a line that crosses the central axis at a right angle. The moment grows with the distance d to the axis, and the product R · M(P) does not change: it is the invariant I.
